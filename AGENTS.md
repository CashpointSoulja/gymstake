# GymStake working agreement

I treat GymStake as an independent product concept owned by the repository owner. I keep the public UI, comments, documentation, commit messages, and package metadata free of authoring-tool or model attribution.

I use TypeScript, integer pence, Europe/London calendar weeks, and immutable weekly commitments. All funds are simulated. I keep real payment handling behind a future adapter and do not introduce payment keys or real transfers.

I use separate feature lanes for design, location verification, settlement, simulation, documentation, and QA. I avoid editing another lane's files without coordinating first. I verify money conservation, idempotent settlement, time boundaries, location rejection, duration qualification, and pending preferences before publishing.

I document product and engineering decisions in first person. I keep scratch artifacts in work/, never commit secrets, and publish only the final checked source.

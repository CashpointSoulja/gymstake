# Explain it like I’m five

I made GymStake like a promise jar for going to the gym.

I choose how many days I want to go this week. I put some pretend coins in the jar. When I go to my chosen gym, my phone checks that I’m there. I need to stay long enough for the visit to count: at least 30 minutes that the app can verify.

If I keep my promise, I get my pretend coins back. People who miss their promise leave their pretend coins in a shared jar. Everyone who kept their promise shares those coins equally.

I can count one visit each day. My week ends when Sunday finishes in London. If the app is closed at that moment, it works out the result when I next open it.

I use pretend people to make the shared jar and leaderboard interesting. I label them as simulated. Every coin is pretend too: I am never charged, and I cannot withdraw a balance.

If I change my gym or my promise, I use the new choice next week. That keeps this week’s promise fair.

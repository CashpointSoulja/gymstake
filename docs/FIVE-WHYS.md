# Five whys

## Why I want to build this

**1. Why do I want a gym commitment app?**

I want to turn a vague intention to exercise into a concrete weekly action.

**2. Why isn’t an intention enough?**

I can postpone a visit repeatedly when today has no clear consequence. A weekly deadline makes progress and the remaining opportunities visible.

**3. Why add a stake?**

I think a stake can make the commitment feel consequential and give the week a clear outcome. I treat that as a hypothesis, not a proven behaviour change.

**4. Why verify attendance?**

I want a check-in to represent a real visit. A tap alone would make the commitment easy to bypass, so I require location evidence and a meaningful duration.

**5. Why begin with play money?**

I need to find out whether people understand the rules, trust the interface, and want to return before taking on the operational and legal responsibilities of real funds.

## What I learned from this chain

I am testing follow-through and rule comprehension. I am not measuring workout intensity or promising improved health. I use a simple weekly target, a modest stake range, a visible deadline, and explicit simulation labels.

I also recognise that money is only one possible motivator. I will compare it with streaks, reminders, social accountability, and forgiving recovery paths before deciding how to evolve the product.
